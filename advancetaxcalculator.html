FY 2026-27 (AY 2027-28) • New Regime
What is it?

Normally you pay tax after the year ends. Advance tax means paying it during the year, in four parts, if your total tax for the year — after TDS — is likely to exceed ₹10,000.

Who does this catch?

Mostly people who switched jobs, freelance on the side, sold shares/property, or earn rent/interest — since a single employer's TDS usually already covers a single steady salary.

What if I'm late?

The department charges 1% interest per month on the shortfall — not a penalty, just the cost of paying late. Sections 4 & 5 below work this out for you automatically.

💡 Tips to Avoid Interest

Pay at least the safe-harbor percentage (12% / 36% / 75% / 100%) of your total estimated tax by each due date. If your income changes mid-year — bonus, new job, a share sale — revisit this calculator and top up the next instalment rather than waiting till March.
